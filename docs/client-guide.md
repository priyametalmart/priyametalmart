# Managing the Priya Metal Mart website

Everything on the website is edited in **Shopify** (admin.shopify.com, or the Shopify app on your phone).
Changes appear on the website **within about an hour**, and you don't need to contact the developer.

## Products

**Products** in the left menu.

- **Change a price:** open the product, then edit **Price**. **Compare-at price** is the MRP, which shows as the crossed-out price and the "% off" label.
- **Add sizes:** in **Variants**, choose **Add options like size or color**. Name it "Size" and add each size, then set a price for each one. Customers will see a size picker.
- **Photos:** drag new photos into **Media**. The first photo is the main one.
- **Description:** type it in **Description**.
- **Stock:** turn on **Track quantity** and enter how many you have. At 0, the website shows "Sold out".
- **Hide a product:** set **Status** to **Draft**.
- **Add a new product:** click **Add product**. Under **Publishing**, make sure **Priya Metal Mart Headless** is ticked, and add it to a collection.

Products we need you to review have tags. Search by tag in Products:

- `sizes-pending`: this product comes in several sizes. Add each size with its price.
- `check-mrp`: the MRP looks wrong. Please check it.
- `mrp-missing`: no MRP yet.

## Categories and Best sellers

**Products > Collections.** Each category on the website is a collection. To choose the four
products shown under **Best sellers** on the home page, add or remove products in the **Best Sellers** collection.

## Home page banners

**Content > Metaobjects > Home banner.**

- **hero:** the large banner at the top. You can edit the heading, text, button label and image.
- **Promo cards:** the two cards below the banner. Each card links to the collection you choose.

To add another promo card, click **Add entry**, set **Placement** to "Promo card", and choose a collection.
**Order** sets the position. Lower numbers show first.

## Contact details

**Content > Metaobjects > Site settings > main.** Fill in phone, WhatsApp, email, shop address and
return period. They appear in the footer, on the Contact page, and on the home page.

## Policies

**Settings > Policies.** Write or generate the refund, privacy, shipping and terms policies. They appear on the
website automatically.

## Orders

Orders appear under **Orders**, and Shopify emails you for each new one. Customers get order confirmation and
tracking emails from Shopify.
